Factors Shaping Our Ideology

Social Equality

Protecting minority rights and LGBTQ rights ensures every individual retains fundamental agency without societal oppression.

Economic Access

Publicly funding health assistance and education creates a fair baseline where equal opportunity can truly thrive.

Human Nature

Individuals are naturally free, equal, and independent. Legitimate power exists strictly through the consent of the governed.

Key Policy Positions

Universal Healthcare & Free Medical Assistance

Position: Guarantee medical care so no citizen suffers financially due to illness.

Action Plan: Expand public coverage for essential treatments, preventive care, and emergency services.

Voter Appeal: Ensures individual health struggles do not strip away a person's freedom and independence.

Accessible Public Education

Position: Fully fund public education to lower out-of-pocket tuition costs and improve resources.

Action Plan: Increase school grants, expand university funding, and ease debt burdens on students.

Voter Appeal: Empowers every individual with the tools needed to succeed in modern society.

Minority & LGBTQ Rights Protection

Position: Codify robust protections against discrimination for all minority and LGBTQ citizens.

Action Plan: Enforce strict equal-rights legislation in employment, housing, and public services.

Voter Appeal: Upholds the fundamental truth that all humans are born free and equal.

John Locke's Core Foundation

"Men being, as has been said, by nature, all free, equal, and independent, no one can be put out of this estate, and subjected to the political power of another, without his own consent."

— Second Treatise of Government (1689)
